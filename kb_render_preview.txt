## Location & Transport
- Nearest metro: Helsingin Yliopisto
- Nearby trams: 2, 3, 5, 6, 6T, 9
- Walk from Central Station: About 10 minutes from Central Station.
- Airport trains: I and P commuter trains to Helsinki Central Station (approx. 28–33 min).
- Airport buses: 600 and 415N to Rautatientori / Elielinaukio (approx. 45–50 min).
- Taxi note: Fixed-price taxis available at arrivals level at HEL.
